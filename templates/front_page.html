🏦 About Orethan Microfinance

Empowering Dreams, Building Futures

📖 Our Story

Orethan Microfinance was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to provide accessible, affordable, and reliable financial services to small business owners, entrepreneurs, and groups who are underserved by traditional banks. We understand the challenges of growing a business in Tanzania, and we are committed to being your trusted financial partner.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower Tanzanian entrepreneurs and small businesses by providing quick, flexible, and affordable microfinance solutions that help them grow and succeed.

👁️ Our Vision

To become Tanzania's most trusted microfinance institution, recognized for our customer-centric approach and commitment to community development.

💪 Why Choose Us?

✅ Fast Approval (24-48 hrs) ✅ Low Interest Rates ✅ Flexible Repayment ✅ No Hidden Fees ✅ Group Loans Available ✅ Mobile Money Support ✅ Dedicated Support Team ✅ Transparent Process

📊 Our Impact

Since our founding, we have helped over 5,000 clients achieve their business dreams. We have disbursed over Tsh 50 Billion in loans, creating thousands of jobs and supporting local economic growth across Tanzania.
